FAQ

Questions we are often asked

How quickly can you start?

A partner can usually meet you within a week. Diagnostics typically start two to three weeks after scoping, once data access is agreed.

What size of organisation do you work with?

Most clients have revenue between $250M and $15B. We also run 100-Day Resets for single sites and business units within larger groups.

How are your fees structured?

Diagnostics are fixed fee. Transformation programmes are fixed fee with up to 30% at risk against KPIs agreed with your finance team before work begins.

Do you work under NDA?

Always. We sign mutual NDAs before any data is shared, and we never name clients without written consent.

Will our people be involved, or do you do the work?

Both. Our teams work on the floor beside yours. Every programme includes capability building so your managers run the improved system after we leave.

Do you work outside your office locations?

Yes. We have delivered programmes in 38 countries and staff teams by sector expertise, not by geography.
