Our approach

Diagnose in weeks. Deliver in months. Sustain for years.

We do not hand over a deck and leave. Two thirds of our hours are spent on the shop floor, in the warehouse and in the planning room.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    3 weeks

    A fact base built from your ERP, MES and shop-floor observation. We size the prize line by line and agree the baseline with Finance before any work starts.

  2. 02

    Design

    4 to 6 weeks

    Future-state processes, KPIs and an initiative charter for every lever, co-designed with the managers who will run them.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    3 to 9 months

    Pilot, prove, scale. Our consultants work on the floor beside your teams, with a weekly results cadence and a stage-gated value tracker.

  4. 04

    Sustain

    Ongoing

    Leader standard work, tiered performance meetings and capability building, so results are still there two years after we leave.
