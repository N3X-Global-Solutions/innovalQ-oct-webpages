The programme

Four phases. One quarter. Results your board can see.

Each phase ends with a gate review with your executive sponsor. Nothing moves forward until the numbers are agreed.

  1. Days 0 to 10

    Baseline

    Data pull, gemba walks and a value map. One agreed number for cost, service and cash.

    • Signed-off baseline
    • Top 20 loss tree
    • Reset charter

  2. Days 11 to 40

    Quick wins

    Stabilise service and capture no-capex savings: scheduling, staffing, inventory policy.

    • Daily tier meetings live
    • First $ captured
    • Service recovered

  3. Days 41 to 80

    Redesign

    Rebuild the two or three processes that drive most of the loss, then pilot and scale.

    • Future-state SOPs
    • Pilot results
    • Scale plan

  4. Days 81 to 100

    Lock-in

    Hand over a performance rhythm, KPI tree and capability plan your leaders own.

    • KPI tree & dashboards
    • Leader standard work
    • 12-month roadmap
