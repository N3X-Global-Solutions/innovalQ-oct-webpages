About the firm

Operators first. Consultants second.

Northbeam was founded in 2009 by plant managers and supply-chain leaders who had been on the receiving end of too many consulting slide decks. We built the firm we wished we could have hired.

Our story

From one plant in Sheffield to 140 transformations worldwide

Our first engagement was a single stamping plant losing £9M a year. Six months later it was profitable, and its plant manager is now one of our partners.

Since then we have stayed deliberately focused. We do operations: how work flows through factories, warehouses, hospitals and back offices, and how that flow shows up in cost, cash and service.
