Manufacturing

Tier-1 automotive supplier, 3 plants, Germany and Czechia

Turning around three stamping and assembly plants in 26 weeks

Challenge, approach and result

The challenge

Two OEM customers had placed the supplier on quality watch. OEE sat at 54%, changeovers averaged 94 minutes and premium freight was running at $3.1M a year.

Our approach

  • Loss-tree diagnostic across 41 presses and 12 assembly cells
  • SMED programme cut changeovers from 94 to 31 minutes
  • Tiered daily performance meetings from line to plant director
  • Root-cause problem solving on the top 10 scrap drivers

The result

OEE rose from 54% to 72%, scrap fell 41% and premium freight was eliminated. Both customers lifted the quality watch within five months.
