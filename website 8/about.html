About Kavach

Your Advocate,
Not the Insurer's

An insurer's agent sells you that insurer's policy. As a broker, we represent you: we compare the market, negotiate terms and fight your corner when you claim.

Our Story

Started in Mumbai in 2012, after a claim that should have been paid

Our founder spent a decade on the insurer side and watched too many small manufacturers lose claims over a missed clause or an under-declared sum insured. Kavach was set up as a licensed insurance broker to fix that, by sitting on the business owner's side of the table.

Today we place commercial property, liability and business interruption cover for factories, warehouses, retailers, offices and hotels across India, from four offices and a team of risk and claims specialists.

What We Stand For

Three principles guide every policy we place.

Independence

We are not tied to any insurer. Our recommendation is the policy that best fits your risk, not the one that pays us most.

Transparency

Side-by-side quotes, plain-language summaries of exclusions, and clear disclosure of how we are paid.

Claims First

A policy is only worth what it pays. Our dedicated claims team handles surveyors, paperwork and follow-ups for you.
