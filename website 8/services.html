Property Insurance

Fire & allied perils cover under the Standard Fire & Special Perils (SFSP) policy. It pays to repair or replace your building, plant & machinery and stock when they are damaged by an insured event.

What's covered

  • Fire, lightning & explosion
  • Storm, flood & inundation
  • Earthquake (as add-on)
  • Riot, strike & malicious damage
  • Building & interiors
  • Plant, machinery & stock

Typical for: factories, warehouses, showrooms and owned or leased offices.

Liability Insurance

When a customer, visitor or third party holds your business responsible for injury, damage or financial loss, liability cover pays legal defence costs and compensation awarded against you.

What's covered

  • Public liability
  • Product liability
  • Directors & Officers (D&O)
  • Professional indemnity
  • Legal defence costs
  • Third-party property damage

Typical for: retailers, hotels and restaurants, manufacturers, consultants and company boards.

Business Interruption / Loss of Profits

A fire can shut your doors for months. Business interruption (also called Fire Loss of Profits) replaces the gross profit you lose and keeps paying fixed costs like salaries and rent while you rebuild.

What's covered

  • Loss of gross profit
  • Standing charges & wages
  • Increased cost of working
  • Loss of rent
  • Indemnity period of 3–24 months
  • Key supplier / customer extension

Typical for: manufacturers, logistics hubs and hospitality businesses with high fixed costs.
