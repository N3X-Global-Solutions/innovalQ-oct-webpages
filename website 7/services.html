Fair Market Value (FMV) Operating Leases

For businesses requiring rapid technology refreshes every 2 to 4 years without technological obsolescence risk. You pay strictly for the use of the equipment over the term, resulting in significantly lower monthly installments.

Monthly Outlay
15-25% Lower
Lease Term
12 to 60 Mo
End of Term
3 Flexible Paths
End of Term Options:
Option 1 - Upgrade: Return the equipment and swap for latest generation model without cash penalty.
Option 2 - Purchase: Buy out the machine at its then-current fair market residual value.
Option 3 - Walk Away: Simply surrender the unit with no lingering debt on balance sheet.

Best Suited For

  • • Healthcare & Diagnostics: MRI machines, CT scanners, digital X-ray, dental imaging, surgical lasers.
  • • IT Infrastructure: Enterprise servers, networking switches, workstations, telecom hardware.
  • • Commercial Printing & Packaging: Digital presses, automated cartoners, labeling lines.
