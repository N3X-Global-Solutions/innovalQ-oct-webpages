Equipment Loans & Hire-Purchase

A term loan secured by the equipment you buy. You are the owner from day one, claim depreciation and interest as expenses, and the hypothecation is removed once the final EMI is paid.

Funding
Up to ₹50 L
Tenure
12 to 84 months
Down payment
₹0 available
  • Tax efficient: claim Section 32 depreciation plus the interest component of every EMI.
  • Soft costs bundled: GST, freight, installation, tooling and training up to 20% of the asset value.
  • Fixed rates: EMIs that do not move when the repo rate changes.

Typical assets: CNC machining centres, press brakes, laser cutters, excavators, loaders, cranes, tippers, trailers and reefer trucks.

Operating Leases

Pay only for the use of the asset over the lease term. Rentals are a fully deductible operating expense, and the lessor carries the residual value, so your monthly outflow is typically 15% to 25% lower than a loan.

Monthly outflow
15 to 25% lower
Lease term
12 to 60 months
End of term
3 options
  • Upgrade: return the asset and move to the latest model without a cash penalty.
  • Purchase: buy the asset at its fair market value at the end of the term.
  • Return: hand the asset back with nothing left on your balance sheet.

Typical assets: Diagnostic imaging, dental and lab equipment, servers and IT infrastructure, printing and packaging lines.

Documentation guide

What lenders need for a fast sanction

Keep your application moving. These are the exact document sets our credit desk asks for.

Application-only

Tier 1: up to ₹25,00,000

  • One-page online application
  • Dealer quotation or proforma invoice
  • Last 6 months of current account statements
  • KYC: PAN, Aadhaar and GST certificate

Decision time: 2 to 4 business hours

Full financials

Tier 2: ₹25,00,000 to ₹50,00,000

  • Last 2 years of ITRs with computation
  • Audited balance sheet and P&L
  • GST returns (GSTR-3B) for 12 months
  • Equipment specification and dealer bank details

Decision time: 24 to 48 hours
