How depreciation lowers the real cost of equipment

As the owner under an equipment loan you claim depreciation from the year the machine is put to use, even when it is 100% financed. The estimate above assumes the asset is used for more than 180 days in year one. Confirm your position with your chartered accountant.

1. Normal depreciation

15% WDV

2. Additional depreciation

+20%
