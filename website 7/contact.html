FAQ

Questions we hear every day

How quickly will someone respond to my enquiry?

An advisor calls back within 2 business hours between 9:30 am and 6:30 pm IST, Monday to Saturday. Enquiries received after hours are answered first thing the next working morning.

Do you charge a fee for arranging finance?

In most cases no. We are paid a commission by the lender. Where a complex structure such as a sale and leaseback needs an independent valuation, we tell you the cost in writing before anything is ordered.

Will checking my eligibility affect my CIBIL score?

No. Our initial assessment uses a soft enquiry and your bank statements. A lender only runs a hard bureau check once you choose to proceed with their offer.

Which documents should I keep ready?

For loans up to ₹25 lakh: PAN, Aadhaar, GST certificate, six months of current account statements and the dealer quotation. Larger loans also need two years of ITRs and audited financials.

Do you work with businesses outside Mumbai?

Yes. We arrange finance in 22 states through our lender network, with desks in Mumbai, Pune, Ahmedabad and Chennai and digital documentation for everywhere else.

Can new businesses or first-time buyers apply?

Yes. Several of our NBFC partners fund businesses with under two years of vintage, usually with a higher down payment or a guarantor. Tell us about your contracts and we will find the right fit.
