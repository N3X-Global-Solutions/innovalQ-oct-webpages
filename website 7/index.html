Frequently Answered Questions

Commercial Equipment Financing Essentials

What credit score is required for equipment financing?

Our Prime Tier programs start at 650+ FICO with the lowest rates. However, because we syndicate across 75+ specialized equipment lenders, we also offer competitive asset-backed approvals down to 580 FICO. Since the equipment itself serves as collateral, approval rates are significantly higher than uncollateralized bank loans.

Can I finance used equipment or machinery from private sellers?

Yes! We finance both new and used commercial equipment up to 15 years old (and older for heavy industrial machines with proven residual value). We finance equipment from authorized dealerships, commercial auctions (e.g., Ritchie Bros), and verified private party sellers.

How does the Section 179 tax deduction work on financed equipment?

Under IRS Section 179, your business can deduct the full purchase price of qualifying equipment in the tax year it is acquired and placed in service. This applies even if you finance 100% of the cost with ₹0 down, resulting in tax savings that often exceed the first year's monthly finance payments!

What is the difference between a direct bank and a broker?

A direct bank only offers its own proprietary balance-sheet products with rigid credit parameters. If your company doesn't fit their exact risk box, you are denied. As an independent brokerage, ApexVanguard maintains syndicated lending relationships with 75+ commercial banks, credit unions, and institutional private funds, allowing us to find the exact lender that aggressively wants your industry and asset class.
