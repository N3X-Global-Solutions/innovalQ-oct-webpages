FAQ

Equipment financing essentials

What CIBIL score do I need for equipment finance?

Prime programmes start at a CIBIL score of 700+ with the lowest rates. Because we work with 75+ banks and NBFCs, we also arrange asset-backed approvals from 650. The equipment itself is the security, so approval rates are much higher than for unsecured loans.

Can I finance used machinery or buy from a private seller?

Yes. We finance new and used commercial equipment up to 15 years old (older for heavy industrial machines with a proven resale value), bought from authorised dealers, auctions or verified private sellers, subject to a valuation report.

How does depreciation work on financed equipment?

You are the owner from day one under an equipment loan, so you can claim depreciation under Section 32 of the Income Tax Act, plus the interest component of each EMI as a business expense, even when the asset is 100% financed.

How is a broker different from going to my bank directly?

A bank can only offer its own products and credit policy; if you fall outside it, you are declined. As an independent broker we compare offers from 75+ lenders, so we can find the one that actively wants your industry and asset class. Our fee is paid by the lender in most cases.
