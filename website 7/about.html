About ApexVanguard

Brokers who used to be the bankers

We spent our early careers saying no to good businesses because they did not fit one bank’s policy. ApexVanguard exists so that never has to happen to you.

Our story

Built to fix the way Indian MSMEs buy equipment

In 2014 our founders were running equipment-finance desks at large lenders. They watched profitable contractors and manufacturers get declined for reasons that had nothing to do with their ability to repay: one bank did not lend on used machines, another wanted property as collateral, a third was simply slow.

ApexVanguard Capital was set up to sit on the borrower’s side of the table. We prepare one lender-ready file, send it to the institutions that actually want your asset class, and negotiate the result. Today a team of 60 across Mumbai, Pune, Ahmedabad and Chennai does exactly that, every day.

What we stand for

Three promises we make to every client

Radical transparency

Every offer is shown side by side with its true cost: rate, processing fee, margin money and prepayment terms. If a lender adds a charge later, we fight it.

Speed that respects your schedule

Equipment delays cost money. Our credit desk works to a 2-hour decision target and tracks every file until the dealer confirms delivery.

Partners, not salespeople

We are paid by lenders, not by pushing products. That frees us to recommend a smaller loan, a lease, or waiting a quarter when that is right for you.
