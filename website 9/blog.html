UPI is now 70% of your sales. Here's how to reconcile it without losing a weekend.

Thousands of ₹200 payments, settlement batches that don't match your POS, and refunds that show up three days later. A step-by-step routine our bookkeepers use for cafés and shops.

Read the key steps
  1. Download the settlement report from your payment aggregator, not just the bank statement.
  2. Match settlements to POS day-end totals, then the bank credit to the settlement.
  3. Book MDR and refunds as separate lines so your margin is real.
  4. Flag any gap above ₹500 and raise it with the aggregator within 7 days.
