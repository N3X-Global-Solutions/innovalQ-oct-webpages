Bookkeeper vs Fractional CFO — which do I need?

Most businesses start with books and add a CFO when they grow.

Bookkeeper
Looks back: "What happened?"
  • →You are spending weekends on receipts
  • →GST filings feel stressful every month
  • →You don't know last month's real profit
  • →Revenue under ~₹25 L a month
Try: Starter or Growth
Fractional CFO
Looks ahead: "What should we do next?"
  • →Planning a new outlet, product or city
  • →Raising funds or applying for a bank loan
  • →Growing sales but cash still feels tight
  • →Investors or partners want monthly MIS
Try: CFO Plus
