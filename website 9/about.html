Our story

Vikram and Anita met while auditing small companies in Bengaluru. Year after year they saw the same thing: owners working 14-hour days, filing GST in a panic and finding out their real profit eleven months too late.

Big firms were too expensive. Freelancers came and went. So they built the service they wished existed: a dedicated bookkeeper, a fixed monthly fee, books closed by the 7th, and a real CFO a phone call away.

Today Hisaab looks after cafés, clinics, D2C brands, agencies and startups in 38 cities, still with the same promise: clear numbers, in plain English, every month.

What we stand for

Three promises we make to every client, from a one-person studio to a 40-outlet chain.

Trust, earned monthly

No lock-in contracts. NDA on day one, read-only bank access and an encrypted client portal. If we don't earn it, you can leave.

Plain English, always

Every report comes with short notes on what changed and why. If you need an accounting dictionary, we've failed.

On time, every time

Books closed by the 7th and filings drafted days before the deadline. In 2025 we hit 99.4% of our close dates.
