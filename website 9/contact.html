FAQ

Good questions.

Do I still need a CA?+

Yes, for your statutory audit and income tax return. We keep your books clean all year and hand everything to your CA at year-end, which usually makes their work faster and cheaper. Happy to work with your existing CA.

Which software do you use?+

We work in Tally, Zoho Books or QuickBooks. If you already use one, we stay on it. If you are starting fresh, we usually suggest Zoho Books and set it up for you.

Is my data safe?+

Yes. We sign an NDA, use read-only bank access wherever possible, and store documents in an encrypted client portal. Only your assigned bookkeeper and CFO can see your files.

How do I send receipts and bills?+

Upload them in the client portal, forward them to your dedicated email address, or simply send a photo on WhatsApp. We take it from there.

What if I'm behind on my books?+

Very common. We do a one-time catch-up (priced by the number of months) and then move you to a regular monthly plan. We'll also check for missed GST or TDS filings.

Can I switch plans later?+

Anytime. Upgrade or downgrade from the next month. No lock-in, no exit fee.
